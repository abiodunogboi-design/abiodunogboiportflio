About Sunday Ogboi

My journey into data analytics started with a simple question: “What can we actually learn from the data we already have?”

My interest became more practical when my fiancée, who works in sales, started having difficulties making sense of her sales records. The information was there, but it wasn’t organized in a way that made it easy to answer simple but important questions — who was performing well, what was being sold, how sales were changing, and where attention was needed.

Instead of just looking at the numbers, I became interested in finding a better way to organize the information and turn it into something useful. That experience inspired me to build my Sales Management Workflow and Performance Analytics project — a workflow designed to transform raw sales records into structured, analysis-ready information that can support performance tracking and management decisions. Since then, I’ve come to see data analytics as more than a career path. It’s a way of thinking.

I even apply data analytics to my personal life, using data to understand my activities, track patterns, measure progress, and make better decisions. That same curiosity drives my continuous learning, and I ultimately want to bring this enthusiasm and analytical mindset into business — helping organizations turn messy data into meaningful insights, better decisions, and measurable improvements.

For me, data analytics isn't just about finding numbers. It’s about finding the story behind them — and using that story to make things better.
